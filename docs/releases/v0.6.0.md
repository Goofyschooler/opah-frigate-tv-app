# Opah 0.6.0

Opah 0.6.0 introduces a complete visual redesign for Android TV and Google TV.
A glass-style interface puts your cameras first, uses space more efficiently,
and makes everyday browsing and settings easier to navigate with a remote.

## A new look throughout

- **Cameras** is the opening page, with **Activity**, **Clips**, and
  **Settings** in the side navigation. Your saved startup choice is preserved.
- Camera pictures have more room, with compact controls, clear focus outlines,
  and quieter backgrounds.
- System, Dark, Light, and Custom themes all use the new design. Your saved
  appearance choices carry over.
- **Reduce transparency** joins **High contrast** and **Reduce motion** in
  Appearance. Rounded corners are standard, with optional **Extra rounding**.
- Settings, Appearance, server information, Device Info, and About have
  clearer layouts and more reliable scrolling.
- Live-video controls cover less of the camera picture. Recorded playback
  keeps its wide, selectable timeline.

## Less work with the remote

- Return from a camera or recording to your place in the browser, including
  the selected item and filters.
- Press Back to hide visible controls in camera views and playback. Press Back
  again while the controls are hidden to leave the view.
- Choose two to four cameras, then name a saved view. Go back to change your
  selection without losing the name you entered.
- Open Highlights as a clearly identified review queue and continue through
  its recordings with **Next activity**.
- **Next activity** stays within the New or Reviewed list you opened.
- Keep track of the selected Activity item while using its actions. Find
  **Load more activity** at the end of the results.
- Use **Date & time** in History to jump to a day and hour.
- Press right on a Settings category to enter that category's controls.

## Clearer status and recovery

- Camera previews show when they were checked and offer a retry after a
  failure. Camera groups and Monitor keep playback or preview failures
  visible. Retrying one group camera keeps the others playing.
- Failed clip saves and review changes show a message beside the affected
  action, with a retry when appropriate.
- Connection setup keeps your entries while you test the connection or correct
  an error.
- Monitor's timer cycles through Never, 30, 60, and 120 minutes.
- Larger text stays visible in camera descriptions, Settings headings no
  longer overlap scrolling content, and adjustable controls expose clearer
  accessibility information.

Install the update over your existing Opah app to keep its saved connection,
camera views, and preferences. See the
