# Opah 0.5.2

Opah 0.5.2 makes television-remote navigation more predictable and restores
clear controls for recorded video and available updates.

## Remote navigation

- Activity and Clips keep focus in the list while you move through items.
- Motion Search moves clearly between its tabs, cameras, picture areas,
  search action, and results.
- Settings keeps focus on the selected page and returns to the correct section.
- Full-screen camera views no longer let focus move into hidden navigation.

## Recorded playback

- The recorded-video timeline can now be selected with the remote.
- Use left or right on the timeline to move backward or forward by 10 seconds,
  including while watching reviewed Activity.

## Update indicator

- A colored dot appears inside the Settings gear when a newer Opah release is
  available.
